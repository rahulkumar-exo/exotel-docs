# Advanced Configuration

Advanced configuration options for your Exotel integration are managed through your account dashboard at [my.exotel.com](https://my.exotel.com) under **Settings**.

For configuration help, contact your account manager or reach out to [Exotel Support](https://support.exotel.com).
