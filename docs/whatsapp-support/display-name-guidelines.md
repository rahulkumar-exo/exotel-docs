# WhatsApp Display Name Guidelines

Your WhatsApp Business display name is what customers see when they receive a message from your business. Meta has strict guidelines for display names, and choosing the right name is critical for approval and customer trust.

## Where the Display Name Appears

The display name appears in several places within the WhatsApp app:

- **Chat list** -- When a customer receives a message from your business
- **Chat header** -- At the top of the conversation
- **Business profile** -- When a customer taps on your name to view business details
- **Contact info** -- In the customer's contact details

## Meta's Display Name Requirements

### Must Follow

| Rule | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Represent your business** | Must be your business name or a clear variation | "Acme Corp" or "Acme Customer Support" |
| **Consistent branding** | Must match the name used on your website, social media, or business registration | If your website says "Acme Technologies Pvt Ltd", use "Acme Technologies" |
| **Minimum 3 characters** | Name must be at least 3 characters long | "ACM" is acceptable |
| **Maximum 512 characters** | Name must not exceed 512 characters | Keep it concise for best readability |

### Must Not

| Rule | Bad Example | Good Example |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **No generic terms alone** | "Customer Support" | "Acme Support" |
| **No personal names** (unless it is the business name) | "Rahul Kumar" | "Rahul's Electronics" |
| **No generic geographic terms** | "India Services" | "Acme India" |
| **No slogans or taglines** | "Best Deals Every Day" | "DealMart" |
| **No offensive language** | Any profanity or slurs | Clean, professional name |
| **No WhatsApp branding** | "WhatsApp Store" | "Acme Store" |
| **No misleading names** | "Official Government" (for private business) | Your actual business name |
| **No excessive punctuation** | "Acme!!!Store!!!" | "Acme Store" |
| **No all-uppercase** (unless it is the brand) | "ACME ELECTRONICS STORE" | "Acme Electronics" or "ACME" (if the brand is ACME) |
| **No URLs** | "www.acme.com" | "Acme" |

## Display Name Best Practices

### Choosing the Right Name

1. **Use your brand name** -- The name customers already know and search for
2. **Add a qualifier if needed** -- If you have multiple WhatsApp numbers, add context:
   - "Acme Support"
   - "Acme Orders"
   - "Acme India"
3. **Keep it short** -- Names under 25 characters display best on mobile screens
4. **Match your online presence** -- Use the same name across your website, Google Business, and social media

### For Multiple Numbers Under One WABA

If you have separate numbers for different functions:

| Number Use | Suggested Name |
|-----------|---------------|
| General inquiries | "Acme" or "Acme Corp" |
| Customer support | "Acme Support" |
| Order notifications | "Acme Orders" |
| Marketing | "Acme Offers" |
| Regional (India) | "Acme India" |

## Display Name Approval Process

### How It Works

1. You submit a display name when registering your phone number
2. Meta reviews the name against their guidelines
3. Review typically takes **a few minutes to 24 hours**
4. You are notified of approval or rejection

### Verification Requirements

| Business Verification Status | Display Name Rules |
|-----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Not Verified** | Must exactly match the name in your business registration documents |
| **Verified** | Can use a common variation or abbreviation of your business name |

:::tip
Complete [Business Verification](/docs/whatsapp-support/business-verification) before submitting your display name. Verified businesses have more flexibility in choosing names (e.g., using a common abbreviation instead of the full legal name).
:::

## Changing Your Display Name

You can change your display name after initial setup, but:

1. Each change requires a new review by Meta
2. Navigate to **WhatsApp Manager > Phone Numbers > Select Number > Edit**
3. Enter the new display name
4. Submit for review
5. The old name continues to display until the new one is approved

:::warning
Frequent display name changes may trigger additional review. Only change your display name when necessary (e.g., rebranding, correcting errors).
:::

## Common Rejection Reasons and Fixes

| Rejection Reason | Example | Fix |
|-----------------|---------|-----|
| "Name does not represent business" | "Best Service Provider" | Use your actual business name |
| "Name contains generic terms" | "Online Shopping" | Add your brand: "Acme Shopping" |
| "Name does not match business verification" | "Acme Tech" when registered as "Acme Technologies Pvt Ltd" | Use "Acme Technologies" or complete business verification first |
| "Name contains URL" | "Shop at acme.com" | Use "Acme Shop" |
| "Name is too generic" | "Support Team" | Use "Acme Support Team" |
| "Name is misleading" | "Official Banking Service" | Use your actual bank or company name |

## Regional Considerations

### India
- If your business is registered as a Pvt Ltd or LLP, you do not need to include the full suffix
- Use the name customers commonly know (e.g., "Flipkart" instead of "Flipkart Internet Private Limited")
- Hindi or regional language display names are supported with proper Unicode

### Southeast Asia
- Use the name consistent with your local business registration
- Multi-language names are supported
- Consider using English if your customer base spans multiple countries

## Display Name vs. Business Profile Name

| Attribute | Display Name | Profile Name |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Where it appears** | Chat list, notifications | Business profile page |
| **Requires Meta approval** | Yes | No |
| **Can be changed** | Yes, with re-approval | Yes, anytime |
| **Character limit** | 512 | 512 |
| **Visibility** | Always visible | When customer views profile |
