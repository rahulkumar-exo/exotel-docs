## Types of Streams

There are two types of Streams:

### Unidirectional

Unidirectional streams send audio from the call to your server in one direction. Use the **Stream Applet** for this type.

### Bidirectional

Bidirectional streams allow two-way audio communication between the call and your server. Use the **Voicebot Applet** for this type, enabling interactive voice bot experiences.
