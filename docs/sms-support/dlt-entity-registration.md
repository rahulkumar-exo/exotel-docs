# DLT Entity Registration

Entity registration is the first step in the DLT compliance process. You must register your business on a DLT portal before you can send any SMS in India. This guide provides detailed, operator-specific instructions for Jio, Airtel, Vodafone-Idea, and BSNL.

:::tip
You only need to register on **one** DLT portal. Your registration is shared across all operators via the DLT blockchain. Choose the portal that best suits your needs based on the comparison below.
:::

## Choosing a DLT Portal

| Portal | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| **Jio TrueConnect** | Fast approval, intuitive interface, widely used | Occasional high traffic causing slow responses | Most businesses |
| **Airtel** | Reliable, good support | Interface can be complex | Airtel-heavy customer base |
| **Vodafone-Idea (ViLPower)** | Fast approval, simple interface | Fewer support resources | Quick setup |
| **BSNL** | Government entity friendly | Slower processing times | Government organizations |

## Documents Required

Prepare the following documents before starting registration. Requirements are similar across all portals:

| Document | Format | Details |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **PAN Card** | PDF or JPG, max 2 MB | Company PAN (not individual, unless sole proprietor) |
| **Certificate of Incorporation** | PDF, max 2 MB | Ministry of Corporate Affairs certificate |
| **GST Certificate** | PDF, max 2 MB | GST registration certificate with GSTIN |
| **Authorization Letter** | PDF, max 2 MB | On company letterhead, authorizing the registrant |
| **Identity Proof** | PDF or JPG, max 2 MB | Aadhaar, passport, or voter ID of authorized person |
| **Address Proof** | PDF, max 2 MB | Utility bill, bank statement, or registered office lease |

:::warning
All documents must clearly show the company name exactly as it appears in your Certificate of Incorporation. Any name mismatch between documents is the most common reason for registration rejection.
:::

## Registration on Jio TrueConnect

### Step 1: Create an Account

1. Visit [https://trueconnect.jio.com](https://trueconnect.jio.com).
2. Click **Register** on the login page.
3. Select entity type:
   - **Enterprise** -- If you are sending SMS for your own business.
   - **Aggregator** -- If you are a service provider sending on behalf of clients (like Exotel).
4. Enter your mobile number and verify with OTP.
5. Create a password and log in.

### Step 2: Fill Business Details

| Field | How to Fill |
|-------|-------------|
| Company Name | Exact name from Certificate of Incorporation |
| Company Type | Private Limited / LLP / Partnership / Sole Proprietor / Trust / Society |
| PAN Number | 10-character alphanumeric PAN |
| GSTIN | 15-character GST identification number |
| Registered Address | As per your incorporation certificate |
| City / State / Pincode | Match your registered address |
| Contact Person Name | Full name of the authorized person |
| Contact Email | Official business email (not personal) |
| Contact Mobile | Mobile number for communication |

### Step 3: Upload Documents

1. Navigate to the **Documents** section.
2. Upload each document in the specified format.
3. Ensure all files are clear, legible, and within size limits.

### Step 4: Review and Submit

1. Review all entered details for accuracy.
2. Accept the terms and conditions.
3. Click **Submit**.
4. Note down your **Application Reference Number**.

### Step 5: Payment

1. Jio charges a one-time registration fee (approximately Rs. 5,900 + GST).
2. Complete the payment via the portal.
3. Registration review begins after payment confirmation.

### Step 6: Approval

- Jio reviews your application within **3-5 business days**.
- You receive an email notification upon approval.
- Log in to the portal to find your **Principal Entity ID (PE ID)**.

## Registration on Airtel

### Step 1: Create an Account

1. Visit [https://www.airtel.in/business/commercial-communication](https://www.airtel.in/business/commercial-communication).
2. Click **Register as Enterprise**.
3. Enter your business email and mobile number.
4. Verify OTP and set your password.

### Step 2: Fill Business Details

| Field | How to Fill |
|-------|-------------|
| Entity Name | Exact legal business name |
| Entity Type | Enterprise or Aggregator |
| PAN | Company PAN number |
| TAN | Tax Deduction Account Number (if applicable) |
| GST Number | 15-character GSTIN |
| Registered Office Address | Full registered address |
| Authorized Signatory | Name and designation |

### Step 3: Upload Documents

Upload the required documents. Airtel may request additional documents based on entity type:

- For **LLPs**: LLP Agreement
- For **Trusts**: Trust Deed
- For **Societies**: Society Registration Certificate

### Step 4: Submit and Pay

1. Review all details.
2. Submit the registration.
3. Complete the payment (approximately Rs. 5,900 + GST).

### Step 5: Approval

- Airtel reviews registrations within **5-7 business days**.
- Track status via the portal dashboard.
- Entity ID is issued upon approval.

## Registration on Vodafone-Idea (ViLPower)

### Step 1: Create an Account

1. Visit [https://www.vilpower.in](https://www.vilpower.in).
2. Click **Sign Up**.
3. Select **Enterprise** or **Aggregator**.
4. Enter your details and verify via OTP.

### Step 2: Fill Business Details

| Field | How to Fill |
|-------|-------------|
| Organization Name | Exact legal name |
| Organization Type | Select from dropdown |
| PAN | Company PAN |
| GST | GSTIN number |
| Address | Registered business address |
| Authorized Person | Name, email, and mobile number |

### Step 3: Upload Documents

Upload all required documents. ViLPower accepts PDF and image formats up to 2 MB per file.

### Step 4: Submit and Pay

1. Review your application.
2. Submit for processing.
3. Pay the registration fee (approximately Rs. 5,900 + GST).

### Step 5: Approval

- ViLPower typically processes registrations within **3-5 business days**.
- Approval notification is sent via email and SMS.
- Entity ID is available in the portal dashboard.

## Registration on BSNL

### Step 1: Create an Account

1. Visit [https://www.ucc-bsnl.co.in](https://www.ucc-bsnl.co.in).
2. Click **Register**.
3. Select your entity type.
4. Enter your details and verify via OTP.

### Step 2: Fill Business Details

Fill in the same business details as other portals (company name, PAN, GST, address, authorized person).

### Step 3: Upload Documents

Upload the required documents. BSNL may have additional requirements for government entities.

### Step 4: Submit and Pay

1. Submit the registration.
2. Complete the payment (fee varies; check the portal for current pricing).

### Step 5: Approval

- BSNL processing times are typically **5-10 business days**.
- Track status via the portal.
- Entity ID is issued upon approval.

:::note
BSNL's portal may have occasional downtime. If you experience issues, try registering on Jio or ViLPower instead.
:::

## After Entity Approval

Once your entity is approved on any portal:

1. **Save your Entity ID** -- This is your Principal Entity ID (PE ID), a unique numeric identifier (e.g., `1101567890000012345`).
2. **Register headers** -- Proceed to [DLT Header Registration](/docs/sms-support/dlt-header-registration) to register your sender IDs.
3. **Register templates** -- After headers are approved, register templates via [DLT Template Registration](/docs/sms-support/dlt-template-registration).
4. **Configure Exotel** -- Enter your Entity ID in the Exotel Dashboard under **Settings** > **SMS Settings**.

## Cross-Portal Synchronization

Your entity registration is shared across all DLT portals via the blockchain. However, synchronization takes time:

| Action | Sync Time |
|--------|-----------|
| New entity registration | 5-7 business days |
| Header registration | 3-5 business days |
| Template registration | 3-5 business days |

:::warning
If you register your entity on Jio but your SMS is routed through an Airtel subscriber, the Airtel DLT system must also recognize your entity. Allow adequate sync time before going live.
:::

## Common Registration Issues

| Issue | Cause | Resolution |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Application rejected | Document mismatch with company records | Ensure all documents match the legal entity name exactly |
| PAN verification failed | PAN does not match the company name | Use the company PAN, not personal PAN |
| GST mismatch | GSTIN not linked to the registered PAN | Verify GSTIN status on the GST portal |
| Long approval delay | High application volume | Follow up with portal support; typical SLA is 7 business days |
| Payment failure | Portal payment gateway issue | Try a different payment method or browser |
| Duplicate registration | Already registered on another portal | One registration is sufficient; use the existing Entity ID |
