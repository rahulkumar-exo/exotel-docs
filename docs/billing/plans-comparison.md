# Plans Comparison

Exotel offers three plan tiers designed for businesses at different stages. Each plan includes a base set of features with increasing capabilities, higher limits, and dedicated support as you move up.

## Plan Overview

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Target Audience** | Small businesses, startups | Growing businesses, mid-market | Large enterprises, high-volume |
| **Minimum Commitment** | Monthly | Monthly or annual | Annual (custom) |
| **Onboarding** | Self-service | Guided setup | Dedicated onboarding manager |
| **Support** | Email support | Email + phone support | Dedicated account manager |
| **SLA** | Standard | 99.5% uptime | 99.9%+ uptime with custom SLA |

## Choosing the Right Plan

### Starter Plan

Best for businesses that are:
- Just starting with cloud telephony
- Processing fewer than 1,000 calls per month
- Need basic IVR and call routing
- Operating with a small team (1--10 agents)

### Growth Plan

Best for businesses that are:
- Scaling their communication operations
- Processing 1,000 -- 50,000 calls per month
- Need advanced features like campaigns, number masking, and multi-level IVR
- Integrating with CRM and other business tools
- Operating with a growing team (10--100 agents)

### Enterprise Plan

Best for businesses that are:
- Running mission-critical communication workflows
- Processing 50,000+ calls per month
- Need custom SLAs, dedicated infrastructure, and priority support
- Require advanced security features (SSO, audit logs, IP whitelisting)
- Operating large contact centers (100+ agents)

:::tip
Not sure which plan is right for you? [Contact Exotel Sales](https://exotel.com/contact/) for a personalized recommendation based on your use case and volume.
:::

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Log in to [my.exotel.com](https://my.exotel.com)
2. Navigate to **Billing** > **Plan**
3. Click **Upgrade Plan**
4. Select the new plan tier
5. Review the feature differences and pricing
6. Confirm the upgrade

:::info
Plan upgrades take effect immediately. You will be billed the prorated difference for the remainder of the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
:::

## Volume-Based Pricing

All plans support volume-based pricing adjustments. As your usage grows, you may qualify for:

- **Volume discounts** -- Reduced per-pulse and per-SMS rates at higher volumes
- **Committed-use discounts** -- Lower rates in exchange for minimum monthly commitments
- **Custom pricing** -- Tailored rate cards for enterprise-grade deployments

See [Enterprise Billing](/docs/billing/enterprise-billing) for details on custom pricing arrangements.
